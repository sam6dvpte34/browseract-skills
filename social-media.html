Social media Skill catalog

Run social media research Skills with BrowserAct.

A curated set of reusable Skill templates for agents that need to collect profiles, posts, comments, hashtags, creators, and trend signals from real social websites.

Try BrowserAct for social media View the Skill repo

Why this exists.

Plain HTTP scraping often breaks on login walls, JavaScript-rendered feeds, rate limits, and dynamic platform layouts. These Skills keep repeated social research workflows reusable.

Creator discovery

Find relevant creators by profile, hashtag, keyword, topic, or audience signal.

Content collection

Collect posts, reels, videos, captions, engagement counts, comments, and source URLs.

Trend monitoring

Track hashtags, trending pages, music, topics, search results, and ranking pages.

Community research

Collect discussions, replies, group posts, comments, and recurring user pain points.

Coverage examples.

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Reddit, Facebook, Pinterest, Snapchat, Twitch, Discord, Telegram, Douyin, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu, Weibo, Bluesky, and more.
